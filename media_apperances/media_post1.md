Feeling incredibly grateful to the Coalition Against Childhood Cancer (CAC2) for the chance to co-lead the breakout session “Unlocking AI for Impact: Real-World Applications in Nonprofits” at this year’s CAC2 summit, June 16 - 17 in Washington DC.

I’m teaming up with Geoff Still, CEO of the Pediatric Brain Tumor Foundation, to share not just tools, but real stories. Stories of how AI is quietly changing the game for nonprofits, especially those working with families who need us the most.

In the past year, I’ve had the privilege of building systems that help automate donor outreach, personalize messaging, generate grant proposals, and even flag compliance risks before they happen. For a non-profit, recently we built a HIPAA-aware AI layer that protects patient privacy while still allowing us to work faster and smarter. At the Neev Kolte & Brave Ronil Foundation, AI helped us focus more on people and less on paperwork.

This work is deeply personal to me. I’ve seen how hard nonprofits work behind the scenes, how much time is spent on forms, follow-ups, and fundraising. My hope is to show how AI can give some of that time back. Time to connect. To breathe. To do the work that really matters.

If you’re a CAC2 member or a nonprofit leader thinking about AI, whether you're just curious or already experimenting, I’d love to hear what you’re working on and where you need help. Please feel free to reach out. Let’s learn together and build something meaningful.
