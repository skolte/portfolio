Born to Shine is a platform where we spotlight young talent, ages 5-25, who have chosen the path to stand out and sparkle. This week, our show opted for a different angle where we spotlight the Kolte family. Neev Kolte has been diagnosed with a rare tumor referred to as DIPG - Diffuse intrinsic pontine gliomas. DIPG is a type of tumor that starts in the brain stem, the part of the brain just above the back of the neck and connected to the spine. The brain stem controls breathing, heart rate and the nerves and muscles that help us see, hear, walk, talk and eat.His father Sandeep Kolte and our local Pleasanton community leader Sandy Schneider, educate us to build awareness on this rare cancer affecting children and what we can do to help Neev and his family during these difficult times. We appeal to all of you to come forth and give in some way.
